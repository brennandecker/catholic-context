# Founding Reviewer Panel

## Purpose

Record who the founder has manually appointed to review Catholic Context knowledge proposals during the founding stage.

Appointment here is a **project governance** act. It does not claim Church office, diocesan faculties, or Magisterial authority. Reviewers help ensure Catholic Context faithfully represents sources; they do not define doctrine.

## Appointing authority

During the founding stage, the repository founder manually approves every reviewer. See [`docs/REVIEWER_CORRECTION_WORKFLOW.md`](../docs/REVIEWER_CORRECTION_WORKFLOW.md).
